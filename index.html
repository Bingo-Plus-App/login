Safer sign-in checklist

1. Verify the domain

Inspect the full website address, including spelling and redirects. A familiar logo alone is not proof of authenticity.

2. Protect your credentials

Use a unique password and never share one-time codes with people contacting you unexpectedly.

3. Review mobile compatibility

Keep your browser and device updated. Avoid unknown APK files and unexpected browser extensions.

4. Handle account issues carefully

If login fails, review the account help information before trying unfamiliar third-party recovery services.
